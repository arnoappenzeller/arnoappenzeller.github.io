µBrowser 5.0

µBrowser 5.0 brings the web to Apple Watch. Open and search websites, keep favorite pages close, and manage bookmarks from the companion app on iPhone. With Ask µBrowser on supported devices, you can ask questions about publicly readable webpages and receive concise answers with visible sources—right on your wrist.

App Store descriptionEnglish · Version 5.0

µBrowser is the micro browser for your Apple Watch.

Quickly look something up, open a favorite website or read a webpage when you don’t have another device around. Built for short visits on a small screen, µBrowser puts the web on your wrist.

NEW: ASK µBROWSER

Ask questions about webpages, get a quick summary and explore further with follow-up questions. Answers include sources so you can open the original page and keep reading.

FEATURES

  • Open web addresses directly on your Apple Watch.
  • Search with DuckDuckGo, Google, Bing, Brave Search, Startpage or Ecosia.
  • Revisit recent pages and save your favorites as bookmarks.
  • Manage bookmarks with the iPhone companion app.
  • Quickly access the browser through watch face complications.
  • Choose whether to save recently visited pages, how many to keep and whether to include searches.
  • Choose whether to keep cookies between browsing sessions.

PLEASE NOTE

Browsing on your wrist has its limits. Large or complex webpages may not work as expected. JavaScript support is limited, and signing in to websites may not work.

Ask µBrowser works with publicly readable content. It may be unable to read pages behind sign-ins or paywalls, or content that requires JavaScript.

Ask µBrowser requires watchOS 27 or later and available Apple Intelligence and Private Cloud Compute services. Answers are generated using Apple’s Private Cloud Compute. Availability depends on your device, Apple Account, language and region. Daily usage limits may apply.

An internet connection is required.
