# The idea behind Duometry

A phone that can be folded is a fascinating device.
I always looked at other devices and the possibilites and was happy that Apple choose to enter the turf with the Duo.

While the Duo provides a whole new variant of iOS which is exciting by itself, it also adds a whole new layer of interaction when the input can be the hinge of the device or you can interact by folding it.

My first question when I saw the Duo was the same I have with every Apple device or Apple software release: What can you do with this what you could not do before.

I quickly realized that my next app won't be a tool but rather a fun showcase.

The idea was: What if your Duo can be used as angle measruement tool and what will happen if you make game out of this.

The result is Duometry - An angle folding game for Duo and other iPhones

# Introducing Duometry

Duometry is an angle guess game that can be controlled with the phones hinge.

The main premise is: You get an random angle between 0 and 180 degress and you should fold your phone to that angle (or where you think it is).
When you locked the angle in the app measures the differences and you'll receive a score.

This can be played in Solo Mode in 5 or 10 turns or in pass and play multiplayer with up to 8 players.
The multiplayer is also a great way to try out Duo with your friends.

In addition to that the whole game can be played with non Duos. On those phones you use a virtual dial as angle input.

## Features

* Play solo over 5 or 10 rounds and aim for your best score.
* Pass one device between 2–8 players for a friendly competition.
* Explore angles at your own pace with the dial. On hinge devices, Free Measure lets you read the device’s fold angle directly.
* Come back for a daily challenge with three targets. 
* Different daily modes put your angle intuition and visual memory to the test.
