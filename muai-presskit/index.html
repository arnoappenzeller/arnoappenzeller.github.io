µAI

Tiny AI for Your Wrist

µAI is an AI assistant for Apple Watch and iPhone, built for quick questions throughout the day. Ask from your wrist and get a concise answer, then tap for more detail or ask a follow-up. Conversations sync through your private iCloud database, so you can continue on your iPhone. Responses are powered by Apple’s Foundation Models and Private Cloud Compute.

Business modelFree download · $2.99 USD one-time unlock

Try your first question on Apple Watch for free, including one longer answer to that question. Then unlock new questions, follow-ups, and longer answers on Apple Watch and iPhone with one purchase on your Watch. No µAI subscription or per-message fees. Regional prices may vary; Apple’s daily usage limits apply.

App Store descriptionEnglish · Full description and features

Your question. One screen. An answer at a glance.

µAI brings AI chat to your Apple Watch, powered by Apple Foundation Models. Speak or type a question and get a concise answer that fits on a single Watch screen—so you can read the whole answer without scrolling. Explore an idea, understand something new, or find the right words for a message. Want to know more? Request a longer answer or ask a follow-up.

Buy once. No µAI subscription. No per-message fees. Apple's daily usage limits apply. An optional iCloud+ upgrade for more access may cost extra.

  • Answers that fit on one Watch screen, with more detail when you want it.
  • Speak or type your question on your watch.
  • Add photos to your questions on Apple Watch or iPhone.
  • Start chats on iPhone, continue conversations from your watch, and read longer answers comfortably.
  • Search your chat history on iPhone, rename conversations, and delete chats you no longer need.
  • Open µAI from a watch face complication or widget to get to your next question.

Your conversations stay within reach. Saved chats and attachments are stored locally and sync through your private iCloud database. Your questions, relevant conversation history, and photos you add are processed using Apple Private Cloud Compute.

The iPhone app requires iOS 27 or later; the Apple Watch app requires watchOS 27 or later. New answers need an internet connection and Private Cloud Compute availability for your device, region, and language. Previously saved chats remain readable offline.
