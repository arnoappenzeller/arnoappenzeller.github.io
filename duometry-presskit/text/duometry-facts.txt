Duometry — Facts & requirements

App: Duometry · Version 1.0 · Developed by Arno Appenzeller.

iPhone: iOS 17 or later. On phones without hinge input, Guess the Angle uses an angle dial.

Duo experience: iPhone Duo with iOS 27.1 or later and available hinge input. In Solo and Together, match the visible target number by folding; the live reading stays hidden until reveal.

Players & rounds: Solo or two to eight players passing one device. Five or ten rounds per game.

Daily challenges: Three date-defined targets each day: Daily Fold or By Eye, using folding on Duo or the dial on other iPhones. The target disappears before guessing. Weekly completion tracking, saved official results and repeat practice.

Free Measure: Live hinge-angle readings on Duo, with Freeze angle and Resume. Explore Angles on other iPhones uses the dial to set the illustrated angle.

Appearance: System, Light or Dark. Angle and Duo illustration styles can be chosen for measurement and guessing, with a separate reveal-style preference.

Connectivity & storage: Offline play. No accounts, analytics or online multiplayer. Completed solo history, Daily attempts and preferences are stored locally. Daily attempts resume after quitting; active Solo and Together games do not.
